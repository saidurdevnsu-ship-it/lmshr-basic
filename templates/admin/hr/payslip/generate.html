How pay is calculated

  • Gross = basic + house rent + medical + conveyance + other.
  • Absence deduction = gross ÷ working days × (absent days + unpaid leave days). Half days count as ½.
  • Provident fund = basic × PF %.
  • Tax = the employee's monthly tax.
  • Weekends and holidays aren't working days. Days without an attendance record aren't deducted.

After generating

  1. Check the drafts. Add any one-off "other deduction" on a draft.
  2. Select them and use Finalize. Employees then see their pay slips.
